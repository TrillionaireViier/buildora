⚡ The Challenge

A fast-growing chain of 4 barbershop locations was losing potential clients during peak rush hours because receptionists could not answer multiple incoming phone calls simultaneously. Furthermore, over 14% of scheduled appointments ended in client no-shows, causing dead chairs and direct revenue loss for the barbers.

🚀 Delivered Solution

Buildora developed a dedicated Telegram WebApp booking bot synchronized in real time with the barbershop CRM and Google Calendar. Clients select their favorite branch, barber, service, and open time slot, paying a small prepayment deposit directly within Telegram to lock in the reservation.

Delivered Scope of Work

✓
Telegram Web App (TWA) dynamic UI with branch selection and barber portfolio cards
✓
Two-way calendar sync preventing double bookings and slot overlapping
✓
Automated prepayment deposit processing reducing no-shows to zero
✓
Automated SMS / Telegram reminder ping sent 2 hours before the appointment
✓
Barber schedule management dashboard and earnings calculator
✓
Instant rescheduling and cancellation workflows with flexible deposit policies
